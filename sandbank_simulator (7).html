SandBank simulator — single-loop cycle

One refrigerant loop at the core (R1233zd(E)): the data centre's coolant hands heat to the evaporator, the compressor lifts it, and the condenser charges the sand bank directly. A separate loop feeds the evaporator from the DC; a separate thermal-oil loop, controlled by an AI dispatch valve, splits the stored heat across multiple industries.
